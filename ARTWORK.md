# Treasury chest artwork

The teal-and-gold painted fantasy chest was reviewed on 10 October 2026. This app restores that original logo and the matching empty-chest image. Both are reused byte for byte; no replacement drawing was generated. They contain no financial records or personal imagery. Their original content credentials remain intact.

- treasury-chest.png (embedded): f10ea52cc83493a1666d8dcc52e295f2aeb87f940dc9077e179883adc10ee86d
- treasury-chest-empty.png (embedded): d6b23ebd6a8422698bb0e203794ffbe7ee7e3c18b21d2b2f2d0bf683a4039942

Images are embedded in the offline source bundle once and reused for the favicon, logo and header. They are decoration; the chart and labelled amounts convey financial values. Unknown or nonpositive amounts use the empty chest. The build gate accepts only these exact reviewed images and the existing official support mark.

These approved images are embedded in this public standalone build. This release-only repository does not distribute separate image assets.
